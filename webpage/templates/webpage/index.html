ODEEG
Online database for research on the development of vessel shapes and capacities

ODEEG is an open-access online database for research on the development of ancient pottery shapes and capacities. ODEEG is specially focused on 3D models, filling capacities, metadata, and long term archiving of the data, while applying LOD and FAIR principles. It is connected to the wider CVA project. Presently, ODEEG includes fine-wear painted vessels, from ancient Greek territories and Cyprus. Whereas the period encompasses 10th–4th century BCE, approximately from the Geometric period to the Hellenistic period.
